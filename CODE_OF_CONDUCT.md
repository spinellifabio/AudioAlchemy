# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, accept constructive criticism, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@spinellifabio](https://github.com/spinellifabio), through a
private channel on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
